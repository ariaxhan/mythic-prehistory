✅ **20 workstation professions are present: 13 vanilla and 7 modded.**

This is the player-facing index for the shipped Forge 1.20.1 pack. "Buys" means the villager gives you emeralds; "sells" means you spend emeralds or provide the listed input.

## VillagersPlus exact pools

The server config allows at most **two new offers per level** from each pool below. Prices can still move through demand, reputation, curing, and Hero of the Village.

### Alchemist, Alchemist Table

- **Novice:** sells sugar, brown mushroom, or red mushroom for 3 emeralds; nether wart for 5; buys 3 glass bottles for 1 emerald.
- **Apprentice:** sells magma cream for 4; pufferfish or fermented spider eye for 5; a random potion for 5.
- **Journeyman:** sells blaze powder, ghast tear, or phantom membrane for 5; buys a blaze rod for 4 or an ender pearl for 2.
- **Expert:** sells a random splash potion for 8.
- **Master:** sells dragon's breath for 8; glistering melon slice or rabbit foot for 4.

### Horticulturist, any wood Flower Tub

- **Novice:** sells common one-block flowers for 2; buys wheat seeds at 4:1 and beetroot, melon, or pumpkin seeds at 2:1.
- **Apprentice:** sells two leaves for 4; turns 3 sticks plus 1 emerald into a dead bush; buys grass at 4:1 or fern at 2:1.
- **Journeyman:** sells tulips for 4 or a flower pot for 5; buys most saplings for 2 emeralds each.
- **Expert:** sells sunflower, rose bush, lilac, or peony for 5; buys 2 rooted dirt or 2 podzol for 1 emerald.
- **Master:** sells pink petals or blue orchid for 4, wither rose for 8, 2 flowering azalea leaves for 4, or spore blossom for 12; buys 2 moss or 4 bone meal for 1 emerald.

### Oceanographer, Aquarium

- **Novice:** sells sea pickle for 3 or water bucket for 5; buys 4 seagrass for 1 emerald or 8 kelp for 2.
- **Apprentice:** sells any live coral for 3; revives one dead coral for the dead coral plus 1 emerald.
- **Journeyman:** sells any live coral fan for 3; revives one dead coral fan for the fan plus 1 emerald.
- **Expert:** sells any live coral block for 5; revives one dead coral block for the block plus 1 emerald.
- **Master:** sells nautilus shell for 6 or sponge for 8; buys scute for 4, or tropical fish, axolotl, or pufferfish buckets for 5.

### Occultist, Enchanted Basin

- **Novice:** sells small, medium, and large amethyst buds for 1, 2, and 3; cluster for 5; 4 tinted glass for 8; buys 3 shards or 1 glow ink sac for 1 emerald.
- **Apprentice:** sells soul lantern for 4, soul torch for 2, or 2 glowstone dust for 4; buys 2 soul sand or soul soil for 1 emerald.
- **Journeyman:** sells any two colored candles for 4.
- **Expert:** sells 2 quartz for 4, 2 spectral arrows for 2, or 2 plain candles for 4.
- **Master:** sells bottle o' enchanting for 3; buys one poisonous potato for **10 emeralds**.

## Other modded professions

### Aquarist, Aquarist's Table

- **Novice:** buys 10 kelp or 10 dried kelp for 1 emerald; sells a food container for 1 and a fish bag for 2.
- **Apprentice:** sells 4 seagrass for 1, any live coral for 2, and dried-kelp, flakes, mini-shrimp, or pellet fish-food containers for 3, 5, 8, or 12.
- **Journeyman:** buys a filled fish bag for 5 emeralds; sells an amethyst cave decoration for 5.
- **Expert:** buys common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, or exotic fish eggs for 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, or 32; sells a random fish bag or fish tank for 15.
- **Master:** the shipped mod adds no new master-level offers.

### Chef, Stove

- **Novice:** buys tomatoes, lettuce, rice seeds, red or green chilies, and caterpillars.
- **Apprentice:** sells kitchen shovel, iron kitchen knife, stockpot lid, and recipe items for scrambled eggs with tomatoes, braised beef, sweet-and-sour pork, and fish-flavored shredded pork.
- **Journeyman:** buys Dark Cuisine; sells recipe items for pufferfish soup, borscht, and braised beef with potatoes.
- **Expert:** sells soups and advanced recipe items, including pork bone, seafood miso, lamb and radish, wild mushroom rabbit, beef meatball, Dongpo pork, Stargazy pie, Nether-style sashimi, slime-ball meal, and spicy chicken.
- **Master:** sells an enchanted diamond kitchen knife.

### Case Trader, Casing Station

- **Novice:** sells Arrow Loot Case for 10 and Food Loot Case for 15; buys 32 spruce planks for 5 emeralds or 16 string for 3.
- **Apprentice:** sells Tool, Weapon, or Armor Loot Case for 25.
- **Journeyman:** sells Potion or Enchantment Loot Case for 45.
- **Expert:** the built-in Special Loot Case offer remains, but the pack's custom legendary case is exploration-only.
- **Master:** offers 5 Potion or 5 Enchantment Loot Cases for 120 emeralds.

The built-in 120-emerald master offer exceeds a normal 64-item stack and is not practically payable through the standard trade UI. Pack 1.1.0 disables the custom legendary case's villager injection: it no longer appears at novice level for every profession or lets emerald farming bypass exploration.

## Extra trades added to existing villagers

- **Farmer:** Fish of Thieves adds tropical fruit and crop trades.
- **Fisherman:** Fish of Thieves adds its raw and cooked fish, bait, fish buckets, and treasured-fish map trades across the five levels.
- **Cleric, Expert:** Species adds 1 Ghoul Tongue for 3 emeralds.
- **Every profession, Novice:** the former custom Special Loot Case injection is disabled in pack 1.1.0.
- **Wandering Trader:** VillagersPlus and seasonal configuration add modded wandering offers; the configured cap is five offers total.

## Trading rules worth knowing

- Trade once with a villager to lock its profession and current offers permanently.
- An untraded villager can change jobs when its claimed workstation is removed and it can reach another valid one during work hours.
- Workstations must be reachable and unclaimed. Beds do not assign jobs.
- Villagers restock by reaching their own workstation, normally up to twice per Minecraft day.
- Curing discounts, village reputation, demand, and Hero of the Village can change displayed prices.
